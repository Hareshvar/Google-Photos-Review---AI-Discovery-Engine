Key Product Implication from Situational Clustering

High opportunity scores are disproportionately driven by high-stakes panic scenarios (e.g., identity verification at transit hubs) rather than aggregate volume alone. While pet and scenery recall represent significant search frequency, failure in urgent document and milestone retrieval causes immediate reputational trust erosion.
